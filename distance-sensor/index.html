Personal engineering project · Distance reminder

A nudge to sit back.

I wired an ultrasonic sensor to a microcontroller and an LED. When the distance drops below a set threshold, the light turns on.

My role: design, wiring & testingUltrasonic sensor + microcontrollerStatus: working prototype

Why I built it.

I have poor eyesight and astigmatism, and I like gaming. When I’m concentrating, I tend to lean towards the monitor without noticing. I wanted a reminder I wouldn’t have to keep thinking about.

The prototype measures distance and switches on an LED when I get too close. I built it as a personal habit reminder. It doesn’t measure eye health or treat a condition.

01 / The prototype

From wiring to a working reminder.

I connected the sensor to a microcontroller and breadboard circuit, then tuned the distance threshold and alert response through repeated tests.

Measure

The ultrasonic sensor checks the distance between the user and the screen.

Decide

The microcontroller compares the reading with the distance threshold.

Remind

The LED gives a clear visual cue to move back and reset the habit.

This is an educational prototype and personal reminder, not a medical device or a substitute for professional eye care.

03 / What I learned

What I’d test next.

The circuit responds to distance changes in the test clips above. My next step would be to record readings against measured distances, check different seating angles, and find out whether brief movements trigger unnecessary alerts. I’d also like to move it off the breadboard and into a small monitor mount.
