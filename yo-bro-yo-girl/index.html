Yo Bro Yo Girl Youth Initiative · September 2021–present

On the mat.
At the camps.

I’ve volunteered with Yo Bro Yo Girl two to three days a week since September 2021. I coached free wrestling practices for three years and helped run camps focused on gang awareness and giving youth a safe place to spend their time.

← Back to my experience

How I got involved.

I started volunteering in the kitchen at my local gurdwara, twice a week for three years. I also learned harmonium for six years and taught harmonium and tabla for two. I still go back a few times a month to help, including with turban-tying classes.

At Yo Bro Yo Girl, I took on coaching and camp work. The camps included four month-long summers and four week-long spring breaks. I helped with sports, public speaking, and programs about the pressures around gang involvement.
